Customer Ordering Instructions

Help us provide you with the best service by understanding our process and setting the right expectations for your delicious Mandi experience.

Service Scope

  • Radius: Within 5km of restaurant.
  • Min Order: ₹300 required.

Delivery Charges

  • 0–2 km: FREE delivery.
  • 2–4 km: Flat ₹20 fee.
  • 4–5 km: Flat ₹30 fee.

Time & Accuracy

Most deliveries are completed within 30–45 minutes.

Please provide a specific landmark and ensure your GPS pin is accurate to help our riders find you quickly.

Dispatch & Routing

Batching: We optimize routes by batching orders within the same 5km sector to ensure freshness and efficiency.

Payment Options

COD: Our riders carry sufficient change for cash payments.

Digital: GPay, PhonePe, and other UPI methods accepted via QR code.

Delivery Zone Checklist

Distance Expected Time Delivery Fee
0 - 2 km 20 - 25 mins FREE
2 - 4 km 25 - 35 mins ₹20
4 - 5 km 35 - 45 mins ₹30

Important Note

During peak hours (7:00 PM – 10:00 PM) or heavy rain, delivery times may extend by 15–20 minutes. We will inform you at the time of order confirmation.
